🧠 Quarter 2: Living Things and Their Needs

Welcome to Quarter 2! Let's discover the wonderful world of living things - from tiny insects to giant trees, and everything in between!

🌿 What We'll Learn

In this quarter, we'll explore what makes something alive. We'll study plants and animals, learn about their life cycles, and discover what all living things need to survive: air, water, food, and shelter.

🌟 Fun Fact: A honeybee visits 50-1000 flowers in one trip, and it must make about 12 trips to produce a teaspoon of honey!

We'll also learn about habitats and how living things adapt to their environment. Get ready to become a nature detective and observe the amazing living things all around us!
